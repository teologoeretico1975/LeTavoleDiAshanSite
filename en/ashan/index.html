The archive of Sant’Anselmo

In 1247, eighteen crates sealed with black wax arrive at the monastery of Sant’Anselmo di Verdamacht. Brother Sybelius is asked to catalogue what is inside.

He finds one hundred and eleven stone tablets covered in signs that belong to no known alphabet. Sybelius starts the work with a calm mind.

He should have left them sealed.

How it plays

Slide the tiles

Each tablet is a grid of numbered tiles with one empty space. Move them one at a time until they are back in order.

Fewer moves, more stars

Every level awards up to three stars based on the moves you use. If you are stuck, you can ask for a hint.

Read the diary

Once a tablet is rebuilt, the chapter’s symbol is revealed and a fragment of Sybelius’s diary opens.

There is also a new tablet every day, the same for every player.
